#### Hypercom by Justin Moy
- autocrine signaling and paracrine signaling - focus on paracrine 
- current cell-cell communication is biased and cell type annotation introduces bias
- Hypercom - set of tools to score a single cell, ranks ligand-cell interactions
- hypergraph - edges as sets and nodes are contained in the sets
- random walk with restarts models communication - going from one cell to another and seeing whether or not they communicate together to predict ligand receptor interactions
